To generate an online store or web app with AI in TeleportHQ, click + New Project and choose E-commerce or Web App. You describe your idea, pick colors and fonts, and answer a few quick questions. The AI then builds a complete store (products, cart, checkout, customer accounts and an admin panel) or an app with logins, a database and workflows.

Before you start

  • The choice is permanent. A store or web app can't be turned back into a website, and its code can't be downloaded. You can still publish it from TeleportHQ. Not sure? Read What's the difference between a website and a web app?.
  • A store or web app needs a Web App slot. Without one, it runs for 7 days. A trial includes 1 slot, and so does Premium. See Why does my web app need a slot, and what is the 7-day window?.
  • It needs more AI tokens than a website. A web app or store needs at least 20,000 tokens to start. See What uses AI tokens, and how much?.
  • A trial workspace can hold 1 project. See How many projects can I have?.

Generate an online store

  1. On your TeleportHQ dashboard, click + New Project.
  2. Click the E-commerce card.
  3. On the Describe your store screen, describe what you sell and who buys it. For example: A specialty coffee roaster selling single-origin beans, brewing gear and gift boxes to home baristas. Then click Next.
  4. On the Colors & Fonts step, click a color palette and a font pairing, then click Next. (Stores skip the design preferences step and use a light theme.)
  5. If A few fast questions appears, answer each question, then click Continue. See Answer the quick questions below.
  6. On the Your products screen, choose Generate products for me or Upload my own products.
  7. Click Build my store (or Build store with {n} products if you uploaded a file).

A checklist shows each part being set up: design system, authentication, e-commerce, products, admin panel. When it finishes, the editor opens and your store pages generate. The Your store is ready! window then asks how you want to accept payments. See How to accept payments with Stripe or PayPal.

About the products options:

  • Generate products for me: the AI writes a starter catalog from your description, with names, descriptions, prices, categories and images. You can edit or replace every product later.
  • Upload my own products: import a .csv or .json file. A Shopify export works as it is, including variants. The AI reads your columns and matches them to product fields. Nothing is invented. See How to import products from a file (including Shopify).

Generate a web app

  1. On your TeleportHQ dashboard, click + New Project.
  2. Click the Web App card.
  3. On the Business description screen, describe your app and who uses it. For example: A booking app for a yoga studio. Members sign up, book classes, and see their upcoming bookings. Staff manage the class schedule. Then click Next.
  4. On Design Preferences, choose Theme Mode, Animation Level and Page Layout, then click Next. Pick Dashboard layout for sidebar navigation, or keep Standard for top navigation.
  5. On the Colors & Fonts step, click a color palette and a font pairing, then click Next.
  6. On A few fast questions, answer each question, then click Continue.
  7. Wait on the Designing your project screen. The editor opens and your pages start generating.

Answer the quick questions

For stores and web apps, TeleportHQ asks up to 10 short questions so the AI builds the right features, for example "Should customers be able to book without an account?"

  1. Read each question as it appears. It says Pick one or Multiple answers allowed.
  2. Click an answer. To give your own, click Write your own and type it (up to 500 characters).
  3. Not sure? Click Answer for me, or click Let AI answer all at the bottom.
  4. To change an answer, click Edit on the collapsed question.
  5. When every question is answered, click Continue.

You can change these answers later in AI Settings → Onboarding Q&A. See How to give the AI your business details and instructions (AI Settings).

Check that it worked

The TeleportHQ editor opens with a Generating panel that lists the pages being built. On a web app, up to 10 pages are built right away on a paid plan. On a store, the homepage and the store's content pages are built first. The rest of the planned pages appear in the Pages list with a Create button. See What happens while AI builds your site (and if it fails).

Troubleshooting

"A web app needs at least 20,000 tokens to generate. Add more tokens, or continue with a website." Your balance is below what a web app or store needs. Buy tokens in the Purchase Tokens window that opens, or generate a website instead. See How to buy more AI tokens.

"{AI Stores|Web Apps} — coming soon" Web apps and stores are not yet available on your account. The card shows the date they arrive. Click Got it. "Your token balance does not cover this generation. You need about {X} more tokens. Add more tokens, or switch off web app features." The first wave of pages costs more than your balance. Add tokens and click Try again.

"We couldn’t prepare your questions" Click Try again, or click Continue without questions.

"Please upload a .csv or .json file" The products upload takes only CSV or JSON files. Export your products in one of these formats.

"Could not activate the store features (authentication & e-commerce). Please try again or contact support." or "Failed to import design system. Please try again or contact support." Try again. If it keeps failing, contact support from the chat on this help center. See How to get help.

"Your store is missing {features}. Nothing was generated — please try again or contact support." Nothing was built and no pages were created. Try again, or contact support.

Good to know

  • If the AI couldn't decide on some answers, you see "Picked defaults for {n} question(s) where the AI could not decide. You can change them later."
  • The Dashboard page layout currently gives lower-quality results than Standard. Plan for extra polish after generation.
  • If your balance can no longer cover a web app when you reach Design Preferences, TeleportHQ switches the project back to a website and shows the token message.
  • Uploaded products replace AI products. You can't mix both in the setup; add more products later from the E-Commerce panel.
