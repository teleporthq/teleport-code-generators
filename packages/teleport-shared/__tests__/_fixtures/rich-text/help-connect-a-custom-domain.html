In TeleportHQ, you connect a domain you own (like mybakery.com) from the Publish window. Publish your site to Production first, then go to Domains → Custom domain → Configure, type your domain and choose the default address. TeleportHQ then shows the DNS records to add at your domain provider. Once they work, your live site opens on your own domain.

Before you start

  • Plan. Custom domains work on Trial, Pro and Premium. Each of these plans includes 1 custom domain for the workspace. See How many custom domains can I connect?.
  • Publish to Production first. A custom domain always points at your live site, so the project needs at least one Production publish. See How to publish your site.
  • Own the domain. Buy it from a domain provider (for example GoDaddy, Namecheap or Cloudflare) and make sure you can sign in there and change its DNS settings. A DNS record is a setting at your domain provider that tells the internet where your domain should go.
  • Role. You need a role that can publish: Owner, Admin or Designer.
  • One domain per project. A project can have one custom domain, plus its www twin.

Steps

1. Add the domain in TeleportHQ

  1. Open your project in the editor.
  2. At the top right, click Publish. The Publish window opens on the Publish tab.
  3. In the left menu, make sure Domains is selected. At the top, keep Production selected.
  4. Scroll to the Custom domain section and click Configure.
  5. Type your domain after https://, for example mybakery.com or shop.mybakery.com. Don't type https:// yourself.
  6. Click Continue.
  7. If you typed a root domain like mybakery.com, the Choose default domain step opens. Pick one option (the section "Which default domain should I choose?" below explains them), then click Continue. If you typed a subdomain like shop.mybakery.com, this step is skipped.

TeleportHQ shows "Checking if custom domain can be added to your project...", then "Verifying configurations for mybakery.com ...".

2. Add the DNS records at your domain provider

If your DNS isn't set up yet, TeleportHQ shows "Please verify and fix the following missing configurations for your domain and recheck status". Each address gets an Invalid Configuration label and a Type / Name / Value table with copy buttons.

  1. Sign in to your domain provider in a new browser tab.

  2. Open the DNS settings for your domain.

  3. Add each record exactly as TeleportHQ shows it. For a site on mybakery.com with the recommended www option, you add two records:

    Type Name Value
    A @ 76.76.21.21
    CNAME www cname.vercel-dns.com

  4. If TeleportHQ also shows a TXT record with the name _vercel, add it too. Copy its value from TeleportHQ.

  5. If TeleportHQ says "Remove the following conflicting DNS record from your DNS provider:", delete that old record at your provider.

  6. Save the changes at your provider.

For every record type and what it does, see Which DNS records do I need for my domain?.

3. Check the status

  1. Go back to the TeleportHQ Publish window.
  2. Click Check status.
  3. If the table is still there, wait a while and click Check status again. DNS changes often take a few minutes, and can take up to 48 hours.

Check that it worked

The Custom domain section now shows https://mybakery.com with a publish status, and a line such as "Redirect option: mybakery.com". The TeleportHQ Domain row above it shows the badge Redirects to mybakery.com: your free TeleportHQ address now sends visitors to your own domain, and search engines index only your domain.

Open your domain in a new browser tab. You should see your live site.

From now on, the footer of the Publish window has a Publish to custom domain checkbox, ticked by default. Keep it ticked when you click Publish to Production so your domain gets your latest changes.

Which default domain should I choose?

When you connect a root domain like mybakery.com, TeleportHQ adds both mybakery.com and www.mybakery.com and sends one to the other, so visitors land on one main address.

  • www.mybakery.com (marked recommended, selected by default): www.mybakery.com becomes your main address. Visitors who type mybakery.com are sent to www.mybakery.com. You need both the A record and the CNAME record.
  • mybakery.com with a redirect: mybakery.com becomes your main address. Visitors who type www.mybakery.com are sent to mybakery.com. You need both records.
  • mybakery.com with "no redirect": only mybakery.com is connected. www.mybakery.com won't open your site. You need only the A record.

Troubleshooting

"Publish your project to production first — a custom domain always points at your live site, so there has to be one for it to reach." Click Cancel, click Publish to Production and wait until the status says Published. Then click Configure again.

The "Purchase Custom Domain Subscription" window opens when you click Configure Your workspace has used all its custom domain slots. Remove a domain from another project, or buy a slot. See How many custom domains can I connect?.

"Invalid Configuration" doesn't go away Check each record for typing mistakes, wait for DNS to update, then click Check status. For more fixes, see Why is my custom domain not working?.

Good to know

  • Staging never uses your domain. Publishing to Staging doesn't change what your domain shows. See How to test changes on a staging site.
  • Remove a domain. In the Custom domain section, click Remove. TeleportHQ asks "Please confirm that you want to delete the existing domain". Click Confirm. Your TeleportHQ address serves the site again. Remember to delete the DNS records at your provider if you won't use them.
  • When a trial or subscription ends, TeleportHQ removes your deployments and custom domains, so your domain stops showing the site. After you subscribe again, publish again and connect the domain again. See What happens when my trial or subscription ends?.
  • HTTPS. TeleportHQ hosts sites on Vercel, which normally sets up a security certificate for your domain automatically once the DNS records work.
