To activate a TeleportHQ license you bought on AppSumo, open the activation link AppSumo gives you. It opens the TeleportHQ Welcome Sumo-ling! page with your AppSumo email already filled in. Create a TeleportHQ account with a password, or sign in if you already have one. When your dashboard opens, TeleportHQ creates a new workspace on your AppSumo plan.

Before you start

  • You need the activation link from your AppSumo purchase. The page only works when opened from that link. - Use the email address of your AppSumo purchase. TeleportHQ only activates the license for an account with that exact email, and you can't change it on the page.
  • Opening the link signs out anyone currently signed in to TeleportHQ in that browser.
  • An account that already has an activated AppSumo plan can't activate another one.

Steps

If you don't have a TeleportHQ account yet

  1. Open the activation link from AppSumo. The page says "TeleportHQ account for <your email> is almost ready".
  2. Under Please set a password to Sign Up, type a password with at least 8 characters.
  3. Tick I agree to Terms & Conditions.
  4. Click Create account.
  5. Confirm your email: open the email from TeleportHQ and click its link. See How to create a TeleportHQ account.
  6. Answer the welcome questions. The dashboard opens and TeleportHQ creates your AppSumo workspace. The message "Workspace was created with success." appears.

If you already have a TeleportHQ account with that email

  1. Open the activation link from AppSumo. The page says "There is already an account associated with <your email> activation email".
  2. Sign in: click the Sign in with … button you normally use, or type your password and click Sign In.
  3. The dashboard opens and TeleportHQ creates your AppSumo workspace. The message "Workspace was created with success." appears.

Check that it worked

In the dashboard's left sidebar, under WORKSPACES, you see a new workspace. Open its Plans settings to see your AppSumo plan. See I'm on an older, Lifetime or AppSumo plan: what do I get?

Troubleshooting

"The current account e-mail is not the one associated with your activation AppSumo one. Check your inbox and retry this operation." You're signed in with a different email address than the AppSumo purchase. Log out, open the activation link again, and sign in or sign up with the AppSumo email.

"Could not create workspace from AppSumo. Account already has an activated plan." This account already activated an AppSumo license. Check your workspaces for the existing AppSumo workspace. For help, contact TeleportHQ support. See How to get help.

"Could not create workspace from AppSumo. Check your inbox and retry operation." Activation failed. Open the activation link from your AppSumo email again and retry.

The page only shows a loading spinner The page was opened without the AppSumo activation details. Open the full link from AppSumo again, instead of typing the address.

I signed up with Google, GitHub or Microsoft using the same email On the activation page, click the matching Sign in with … button.
